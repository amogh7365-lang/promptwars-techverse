RULES & JUDGING

Individual prompt war. Fair play only. Scores are weighted across five criteria — clarity beats fluff.

Judging Criteria

30%

Prompt Craft

Clarity, structure, constraints, and how efficiently you steer the model.

25%

Output Accuracy

How well the response matches the brief — format, facts, and task completion.

20%

Creativity

Original approaches, clever framing, and inventive use of generative AI.

15%

Relevance

Practical fit to the problem statement and real-world usefulness of the result.

10%

Presentation

Clean explanation of your prompt strategy and confident delivery on stage.

Competition Rules

Solo only

This is an individual prompt war. No teams, no shared accounts.

Approved tools only

Use only the Generative AI tools listed for the event day.

No plagiarism

Copying prompts or outputs from others means instant disqualification.

Fair play

Respect participants, judges, and organizers at all times.

Jury decision is final

Scores from the judging panel cannot be contested after announcement.

Free entry

There is no registration fee. Anyone eligible can join at zero cost.
